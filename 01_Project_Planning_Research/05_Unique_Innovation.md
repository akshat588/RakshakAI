# Unique Innovation

Unlike conventional cybersecurity tools that focus on a single attack vector, RakshakAI introduces a unified AI-driven security ecosystem capable of analyzing multiple cyber threats from a single platform.

## Key Innovations

### Unified Threat Detection

A single platform for detecting phishing emails, malicious URLs, QR code scams, fake job offers, UPI fraud, SMS scams, WhatsApp scams, and social engineering attacks.

---

### Explainable AI

Instead of simply labeling content as malicious or safe, RakshakAI explains why the decision was made, increasing user trust and cybersecurity awareness.

---

### AI Cybersecurity Assistant

An intelligent assistant powered by Large Language Models and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) that answers cybersecurity-related questions using a curated knowledge base.

---

### Risk Scoring Engine

A unified risk scoring system combines outputs from multiple detection engines to produce a comprehensive threat score.

---

### Modular Architecture

Every detection module operates independently, making the platform scalable and easy to maintain.
