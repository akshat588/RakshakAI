# Literature Review

## Introduction

Cybersecurity has become one of the most significant challenges in the digital era due to the increasing sophistication of cyber attacks. Traditional rule-based security systems are often unable to detect newly emerging threats, making Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) essential components of modern cybersecurity solutions.

---

## AI in Cybersecurity

Recent research demonstrates that AI can significantly improve the detection of phishing attacks, malicious URLs, spam messages, fraud, and social engineering attacks by learning complex behavioral patterns from historical data.

Machine learning models provide faster and more adaptive threat detection compared to traditional signature-based approaches.

---

## Explainable AI (XAI)

Many AI-based security solutions behave as black boxes, making it difficult for users to understand why a threat was detected.

Explainable AI improves transparency by providing understandable explanations for AI predictions, increasing user trust and supporting better security decisions.

---

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

RAG combines Large Language Models with external knowledge sources to generate accurate and context-aware responses.

In cybersecurity, RAG enables AI assistants to provide reliable security guidance based on trusted knowledge rather than relying solely on pre-trained model knowledge.

---

## Research Gap

Existing cybersecurity tools often focus on individual threat categories such as phishing detection or URL analysis. Few solutions provide a unified platform capable of detecting multiple cyber threats while combining explainable AI, risk scoring, and AI-assisted cybersecurity guidance.

RakshakAI addresses this gap by integrating multiple threat detection modules into a single intelligent platform.
