# 🛡️ RakshakAI

## AI-Powered Intelligent Cyber Threat Detection & Digital Trust Platform

---

# Project Overview

RakshakAI is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform designed to detect, analyze, explain, and prevent modern cyber threats using Machine Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI).

Unlike traditional cybersecurity tools that focus on a single attack vector, RakshakAI provides a unified platform capable of identifying multiple forms of cyber threats including phishing emails, malicious URLs, fraudulent QR codes, scam SMS messages, fake job offers, UPI fraud, WhatsApp scams, and social engineering attacks.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between advanced cybersecurity technologies and everyday users by providing intelligent risk analysis along with AI-generated explanations and actionable security recommendations.

RakshakAI is designed for students, cybersecurity researchers, security analysts, educational institutions, and organizations seeking an intelligent digital trust platform.

---

# Vision

To become an intelligent AI cybersecurity assistant capable of protecting users from modern digital threats while making cybersecurity understandable, explainable, and accessible.

---

# Mission

Develop an explainable AI-driven cybersecurity platform that combines machine learning, threat intelligence, and large language models to improve digital trust and cyber awareness.
