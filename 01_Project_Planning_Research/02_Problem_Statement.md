# Problem Statement

The rapid growth of digital communication has significantly increased the number of cyber threats targeting individuals and organizations. Phishing emails, malicious websites, fraudulent QR codes, fake job offers, UPI scams, and social engineering attacks have become increasingly sophisticated, making them difficult for users to identify.

Existing cybersecurity tools often focus on a single type of threat and require technical expertise to interpret their results. This creates a gap between advanced cybersecurity technologies and everyday users who need simple, explainable, and reliable security assistance.

There is a need for an intelligent platform that can analyze multiple cyber threats through a unified interface while providing accurate detection, explainable results, and actionable recommendations.

RakshakAI addresses this challenge by integrating machine learning, large language models, threat intelligence, and explainable AI into a comprehensive digital trust platform capable of identifying and explaining modern cyber threats.
